ToS Guard browser extension · Last updated: 2026-05-22

What ToS Guard is

ToS Guard is a free browser extension that uses Google's Gemini API to analyze the legal text of Terms of Service, Privacy Policies, EULAs, and cookie policies for predatory or unusually one-sided clauses, and explains the findings in plain language.

Use of the extension

You agree to use ToS Guard only for lawful purposes and only on pages you have a right to view. The extension is intended for personal informational use.

Your Google Gemini API key

You need your own Google Gemini API key to use ToS Guard. The key is stored locally and used only to call Google's Gemini API on your behalf. You are responsible for keeping your key secure and for any costs Google bills against it. Google's API terms apply to your use of that key.
