Settings

Google Gemini API key

Your key is stored locally in this browser via chrome.storage.local and used only to call Gemini. It never leaves your machine except to generativelanguage.googleapis.com.

Auto-detect ToS pages

When ToS Guard sees a Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, EULA, or cookie policy in your tab, it can pop a small banner asking if you want it scanned.

History & data

Your scans are saved locally on this machine (last 100). Open the History page to browse, search, or delete them.

How it works

  1. Open a Terms of Service or Privacy Policy page (or any page you want checked).
  2. Click the ToS Guard icon and press Scan this page.
  3. ToS Guard extracts the legal text, sends it to Gemini, and shows risky clauses with plain-language explanations.
  4. Risky clauses get highlighted right on the page so you can see them in context.

No data is sent anywhere except to Google's Gemini API using your own key.
